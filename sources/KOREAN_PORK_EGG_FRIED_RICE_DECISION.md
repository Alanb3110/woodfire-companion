# Korean pork / Egg Fried Rice decision log

- Keep the pork and fried rice as two separate recipes.
- Egg Fried Rice is generic by default.
- Gochujang is an optional sauce addition, not a separate recipe.
- Korean pulled pork is an optional protein add-on near the end.
- Bacon is intentionally excluded from the app recipe based on user feedback that it was never used in practice and the pulled pork was sufficient.
