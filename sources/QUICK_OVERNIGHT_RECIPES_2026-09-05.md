## Content decisions

- All recipes remain `qualification: untested` until a real cook validates flavour balance, timing, batch capacity and practical instructions.
- Reference serving size is 4. Maximum serving sizes remain 4–6 depending on grill loading and the geometry of the main protein.
- The recipes deliberately vary flavour families and accompaniments rather than producing ten minor soy/gochujang variants.
- Side dishes and sauces use stovetop, fridge or passive time in parallel with the Woodfire where possible.
- The smoked honey-garlic chicken is the only new meal that deliberately spends extra time in `SMOKER`; the rest prioritise short Woodfire occupancy.
- Generous sauce quantities are retained for satay, smoked honey-garlic chicken and maple-paprika pork steaks.
- Every available recipe receives a dedicated local WebP cover so library browsing and offline use remain intact.

## Safety contracts

Poultry recipes use `FS-USDA-POULTRY-74C` and require 74 °C at the thickest measured point.

Whole-cut beef and pork recipes use `FS-USDA-WHOLE-CUT-63C-3MIN`, with 63 °C followed by an explicit rest of at least 3 min. Thin galbi/flanken pieces and skewered beef are measured at the thickest reliably measurable meat section.

Raw marinade is discarded after contact with raw meat. Where a finishing glaze is wanted, a clean portion is reserved before raw-meat contact.

See `sources/FOOD_SAFETY_TRACEABILITY_V1.md` for authority, jurisdiction and limitations.

## Qualification follow-up

A real cook should record at least:

- actual active-prep time the night before;
- actual Woodfire preheat and cook durations;
- whether stated maximum servings fit without crowding;
- measured internal temperature and rest timing;
- sauce quantity and flavour balance;
- side-dish timing relative to the main;
- any instruction that required interpretation during an active cook.

Only then should a recipe move from `untested` toward `test_cooked` or `validated`.
